# Shedly testing notes

Thank you for testing Shedly. Shedly is a weekly review that makes your list shorter. This build is version 0.11.

## Why we're testing

Shedly is meant to be a weekly habit. We need to know whether people come back, what they shed, and which screens confuse them. What you do shapes the next version.

## What's shared, if you say yes

- The screens you open and the buttons you press (shed, keep, import, the workshop).
- Your machine, theme and cut choices.
- Your streak and counts, shown in ranges.
- The app version and whether Shedly is installed on your phone.
- Screen recordings with every word hidden, so we see layout and taps only.
- Crash reports: the type of error and where it happened in the code.

## What is never shared

- Item names, prices, dates or notes.
- Your name, your country, or anything you type.
- Your list. It stays on your phone.

## Who sees it and where

Only the people building Shedly. The data is stored by PostHog in the EU. Nothing is sold, and nothing goes to advertisers.

## How long we keep it

Only while testing runs. We delete it when testing ends, or sooner if you ask.

## Your choices

- Turn sharing off at any time in **You → Share anonymous usage**. Turning it off stops everything from that point.
- To ask us to delete what we've already received, message Liam directly.

## Installing

On Android, open the app in Chrome and choose Install app from the menu. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
